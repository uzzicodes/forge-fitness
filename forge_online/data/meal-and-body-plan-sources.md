# Meal estimates and body plans

Checked 29 October 2026 (Asia/Karachi).

- Generic white bread: USDA SR Legacy food 174924, 29 g reference slice. Bunny’s/Bunes aliases are expressly approximate generic white-bread estimates, not verified brand-label values. Personal saved labels take precedence for the owner.
- Mixed-meal AI: Gemini and Groq receive the same instruction to provide reasonable, explicit portion and recipe assumptions when identity is clear. This is an estimate; neither a picture nor a brand name establishes exact food weight or hidden ingredients. Confirmation is required before logging.
- Gemini: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits . Provider limits vary by project/model; FORGE’s 20-attempt guard is separate. Gemini app subscriptions are not API quota.
- Groq vision: https://console.groq.com/docs/vision . Configured model qwen/qwen3.8-27b. https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits listed 1,000 requests/day, 8,000 input tokens/minute and 200,000 tokens/day for its free plan when reviewed; image/token limits can bind earlier. FORGE adds a separate 60-attempt/user/UTC-day safeguard, with no automatic paid or cross-provider fallback. Requires the user’s eligible key. Live Groq inference is not verified until a real key is connected; adapter behavior is tested with synthetic upstream replies.
- Open Food Facts: https://openfoodfacts.github.io/openfoodfacts-server/api/ . Public lookup only, no uploaded photos or contributions. API data is community-maintained and incomplete. Attribution and ODbL link remain visible. Fixed-host requests, 24-hour positive cache, 5-minute negative cache and conservative per-minute shared budgets protect its published read/search limits. Missing macros are rejected, not turned into zeros. The Bunny’s result checked during research included an unnamed product and a tortilla: neither establishes the exact white-bread variant.
- Barcode reading: ZXing JS, Apache 2.0. Image processing stays in the browser; only a decoded GTIN or explicitly submitted search name is looked up. Decoding and an uploaded synthetic EAN-13 fixture are exercised in the isolated browser test.
- Adult BMI: https://www.nhs.uk/health-assessment-tools/calculate-your-body-mass-index/ and https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/overweight-and-obesity/ . Standard adult boundaries 18.5, 25 and 30; optional NHS lower-background boundaries 18.5, 23 and 27.5. BMI is not body-fat measurement and cannot distinguish muscle. Ages under 18 are outside this app’s profile range. Pregnancy and some conditions require other assessment.
- Gradual gain: https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-weight/managing-your-weight/healthy-ways-to-gain-weight/ . The optional +300 kcal start is within NHS’s stated 300–500 range. It is applied only when selected.
- Activity: https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html . Balanced strength training remains in all plans; optional walking/cycling starts short and builds toward 150 minutes of moderate activity weekly. Waist focus includes core stability without promising spot fat reduction. See https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2023/11/07/spot-reduction--why-targeting-weight-loss-to-a-specific-area-is-.html .
- Energy: existing Mifflin–St Jeor formula and activity factor, +200 kcal muscle-gain goal, maintenance for other existing goals. A voluntarily selected fat/waist-loss goal uses a modest FORGE starting adjustment of −250 kcal, with app bounds (not a clinical prescription). The NHS does not prescribe this exact adjustment. Underweight fat-loss signups are rejected. Protein retains the existing editable 1.6 g/kg estimate. Sex selects the formula constant; workout design follows equipment, experience and goal rather than gender stereotypes.

No personal records, real photos or real provider keys are used by the automated browser fixtures. Production migrations only add three tables and preserve existing records.
