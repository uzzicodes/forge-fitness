## Verification limits

oEmbed and source-page checks establish provider identity, title, public availability and intended exercise variant. They do not prove uninterrupted playback on every phone or region, or certify every spoken cue. These embeds retain original-player controls, creator attribution and a YouTube fallback. Playback needs internet and is governed by the creator and YouTube. The standing calf demo has exact original-title/author confirmation through YouTube oEmbed; no full frame-by-frame technique audit was performed. Instructions in FORGE should use a controlled comfortable range, neutral wrists and a light learning load rather than copying every optional advanced tip in a video.

The original-provider embed pattern does not grant a download/rehosting license. Provider thumbnails are external URLs; the application does not bundle copied YouTube thumbnails.
